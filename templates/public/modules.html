Role Architecture

SAMS System Modules

SAMS provides three dedicated sub-portals engineered around the academic responsibilities of Administrators, Teachers, and Students.

Role #1

Administrator Module

The central control hub that configures the academic environment, manages faculty rosters, oversees course allocations, and analyzes institution-wide submission statistics.

Student Registry: View, add, update, and enroll students into semester subjects.
Faculty Management: Add and assign teachers to specific course offerings.
Global Monitoring: Inspect all assignments and live submission statuses.
Analytics: Submission rates, average marks, and late counts per course.
Access Admin Portal
Role #2

Faculty / Teacher Module

Dedicated interface for instructors to design assignments, attach guidelines, monitor pending reviews, download student solution files, and award marks with remarks.

Assignment Authoring: Set titles, descriptions, max marks, and strict due dates.
Solution Inspection: Download student uploads directly from the dashboard.
Online Evaluation: Score solutions (0 to max) and enter personalized feedback.
Course Broadcasts: Post announcements to all students enrolled in their courses.
Access Teacher Portal
Role #3

Student Module

A streamlined workspace where learners track semester courses, view task deadlines, upload homework in PDF/DOCX/ZIP formats, replace files before due dates, and track awarded marks.

1-Click Upload: Upload files up to 10MB with server-side mime & size checks.
Resubmission: Replace draft uploads anytime before the deadline.
Deadline Alerts: Real-time alerts for impending submissions due in 48 hours.
Gradebook: View marks scored, percentage, and faculty remarks.
