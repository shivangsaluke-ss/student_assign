System Transformation

Existing System vs. Proposed SAMS

Existing Legacy Handling

  • Manual Submissions: Students hand in printed sheets or email attachments that clutter inbox folders.
  • No Systematic Tracking: Faculty cannot easily see who has turned in work and who is overdue.
  • Slow Grading Cycles: Handwritten feedback and marks sheets take weeks to return to students.
  • Lost & Scattered Files: Physical papers get misplaced, water-damaged, or mixed up across classes.

Proposed SAMS Solution

  • Unified Portal: One portal for Admin, Teachers and Students with strict role-based authorization.
  • Anytime Access & Visibility: Real-time status indicators (Submitted, Late, Evaluated, Returned).
  • Instant Feedback Loops: Teachers grade online with marks and comments delivered directly to student portals.
  • Robust MySQL Relational Store: 8 normalized tables with foreign keys and strict constraints.

About the Project

Student Assignment Management System (SAMS) — tagline "Submit Smart. Grade Faster." is designed to modernize academic submissions across universities by replacing chaotic WhatsApp chats and physical paper with a structured, role-based digital portal.

Core Objectives

  • Centralized Submission Portal: Eliminate physical paper waste and lost files through one unified repository for assignments and student solutions.
  • Automated Deadline & Late Tracking: Precise timestamp recording against server deadlines automatically flags on-time vs late submissions.
  • Fair & Transparent Evaluation: Teachers grade work with max mark validation and descriptive qualitative feedback returned straight to the student.
  • Insightful Administrative Reporting: Submission velocity and average score metrics per course offer academic heads complete departmental visibility.

Existing System vs. Proposed SAMS

Feature / Aspect Traditional / Manual System Proposed SAMS System
Submission Medium Paper printouts, physical notebooks, or disorganized WhatsApp chats / email threads. Unified digital portal with secure file upload supporting PDF, DOCX, ZIP, and images up to 10 MB.
Deadline Enforcement Ambiguous submission times; disputes over late delivery. Microsecond timestamp logging with automatic colour-coded status badge transitions (Submitted vs Late).
Resubmission Control Difficult to replace drafts; students re-submit multiple confusing files. One submission per student per assignment; student can freely replace file until the due date.
Grading & Feedback Graded papers handed back weeks later; comments easily lost or damaged. Faculty marks solutions online with real-time feedback; status transitions to 'Returned' when reviewed.
Administrative Oversight No visibility into departmental submission rates or pending teacher reviews. Real-time analytical charts: course submission rates, average marks, late-submission counts.
